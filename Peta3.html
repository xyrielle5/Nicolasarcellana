LUZON


Mayon Volcano
Mayon (Central Bikol: Bulkan Mayon; Tagalog: Bulkang Mayon, IPA: [mɐˈjɔn]), also known as Mount Mayon and Mayon Volcano, is an active stratovolcano in the province of Albay in Bicol, Philippines. A popular tourist destination, it is renowned for its "perfect cone" owing to its symmetric conical shape, and is regarded as sacred in Philippine mythology.[5]
Baguio
Baguio is a highly urbanized, mountainous city in northern Luzon, Philippines, renowned as the "Summer Capital of the Philippines". Located about 1,500 meters (5,000 feet) above sea level in the Cordillera ranges, it features a cool, temperate climate and is famous for its pine forests and rich indigenous culture.
luneta park
Rizal Park, widely known as Luneta Park, is a massive 58-hectare historic urban park in the heart of Manila. Located adjacent to the historic walled city of Intramuros and fronting Manila Bay, it serves as a central hub for national pride, leisure, and historical remembrance for Filipinos.
intramuros
Intramuros is the historic, 0.67-square-kilometer walled city in Manila. Built by the Spanish in the late 1500s, it was the religious, administrative, and economic center of the Philippines during the colonial era. Today, it remains a major tourist and cultural destination.
venice grand canal
Venice is a historic, car-free city in northeastern Italy, built across 126 islands linked by 472 bridges. Famous for its winding canals, gondolas, and stunning Byzantine and Renaissance architecture, this UNESCO World Heritage site offers a truly unique glimpse into a medieval maritime empire.

VISAYAS


Boracay island
Renowned globally, this tropical paradise features powdery white sand that stays cool even in hot weather. The coastline is divided into distinct sections: Station 1 offers luxurious, quieter resorts, Station 2 is the vibrant hub for dining and nightlife, and Station 3 provides a more laid-back atmosphere.
Chocolate Hills
Located in the heart of Bohol, this iconic, surreal geological formation consists of more than 1,200 rolling hills that turn a rich chocolate brown during the dry season. While there, visitors frequently combine the trip with a river cruise and a stop at the Philippine Tarsier and Wildlife Sanctuary to see some of the world's smallest primates."
Cebu

MINDANAO


Siargao Island
a teardrop-shaped island in the Philippine Sea, is globally renowned as the surfing capital of the Philippines. Centered around the vibrant town of General Luna, it features a mix of world-class waves at Cloud 9, pristine island hopping, and a thriving digital nomad and culinary scene
Camiguin Island
a volcanic pear-shaped island in Northern Mindanao, is known as the "Island Born of Fire." Famous for its seven volcanoes, it offers incredible diving, pristine beaches, and natural springs.
Hinatuan Enchanted River
The Hinatuan Enchanted River (also known as the Hinatuan Sacred River) is a world-famous, deep spring river located in Barangay Talisay, Hinatuan, Surigao del Sur on the northeastern coast of Mindanao, Philippines. Known for its flawless, crystal-clear sapphire and turquoise waters that seem to spring from an unseen underground source
tawi
Discover the southernmost province of the Philippines, Tawi-Tawi, which offers pristine beaches, rich Islamic heritage, and stunning mountain views. The capital town, Bongao, serves as your primary base, featuring accessible natural wonders like Bud Bongao and historic Simunul Island just a boat ride away..
Bukidnon
Bukidnon, the "Food Basket of Mindanao," is known for its cool climate, majestic peaks, and vast plantations. Top spots include Dahilayan Adventure Park for thrilling ziplines, Camp Philips for pineapple fields, and Impasug-Ong Communal Ranch—the "Marlboro Country of the Philippines
